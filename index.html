About the conservatory

Classical ballet, taught with patience and precision.

Founded in 2009, Aria trains dancers from their first plié to the professional stage. Our classes are small, our teachers are former company dancers, and our method is the classical one: technique first, expression always.

Whether you arrive at five or at fifty, you will find the same studio, the same discipline and the same warmth.
